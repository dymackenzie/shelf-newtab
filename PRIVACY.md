# Shelf privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Shelf doesn't have a server, so nothing it reads ever gets sent to me or anyone else. There's no sign-up, no analytics and no ads.

It reads your bookmarks to draw the shelf, and when you add, move, rename or remove a book it does that to the real bookmark. The first time it runs it makes a folder called Shelf under Other bookmarks and keeps the books in there.

It also reads the last week of your history to count which site on the shelf you open the most, because that's the book the ribbon goes on, and it looks up a page's title in your history when you add a link without a name. The counting happens on your computer and the only thing it keeps afterwards is the top site and when it was counted. Turning the ribbon off in Settings deletes that too.

Your settings and each book's height and colour are saved in Chrome's sync storage under the book's address, so if you're signed into Chrome with sync on they follow you to your other computers through your Google account. Icons you paste in and the cover colours Shelf works out from them only stay on the computer you're using.

If you paste an image address for a book's icon, Shelf downloads that one image from that site. Some sites won't hand the image over, and then Shelf just shows it from there, unless you click Keep a copy, which is the only time it asks Chrome for permission to that one site.

If you have any questions just open an issue at github.com/dymackenzie/shelf-newtab and I'll get back to you.
